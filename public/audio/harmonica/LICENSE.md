# Harmonica sample license

The WAV files in this directory are sustain samples of a Hohner Super 64 chromatic harmonica from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), created by Versilian Studios LLC and contributors.

Source paths: `Aerophones/Free Aerophones/Harmonica-Hohner-Super64/Sustains/Normal/` at VCSL revision `HEAD` retrieved 2026-07-30. The samples are distributed under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication**. See <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.

Included VCSL source notes: C3, E3, G3, C4, E4, G4, C5, E5, G5, and C6. VCSL's file labels sound one octave above their numeric label in scientific pitch notation, so the deterministic web filenames identify their measured sounding pitch (C4 through C7). Filenames were shortened; audio content is otherwise unmodified.
