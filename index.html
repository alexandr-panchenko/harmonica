Keyboard Dashboard & Shortcuts

Spacebar Toggle Breath

Instantly swaps between Blow (Exhale) and Draw (Inhale) states. Re-colors the board to match airflow.

Shift or S Toggle Slide

Hold down Shift or tap S to push the slider in. This raises all pitches by 1 semitone.

1 - = Play Holes 1-12

Press numerical keys to activate specific holes: 1-9, 0 (Hole 10), - (Hole 11), and = (Hole 12).

K or P Auditory Aids

Press K or P to replay the sound pitch of the current sheet note without skipping.
