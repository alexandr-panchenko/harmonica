# Manual release checklist

Automated Chromium covers Mode A, score step, relative-ear anchor, synthetic pitch lab, mobile layout, and simultaneous slide/hole pointers. These short checks still require a person and physical instrument.

## Owner validation (about 5 minutes)

- Keep the room silent for 10 seconds after calibration: no note, wandering needle, or staff scribble.
- Play three ordinary low/middle/high notes and confirm note plus cents are sensible.
- Bend slowly once: confirm continuous movement and stable acceptance near the intended pitch.
- Play blow/out, draw/out, blow/in, and draw/in directly, without any state toggle.
- Complete five Find-a-note targets and confirm all five remain visually readable.
- Switch between 10- and 12-hole profiles; confirm hole count, range, and matching-position highlights change together.
- Enable the microphone and confirm the virtual instrument remains clickable; play an alternate pitch and confirm every valid position glows.
- Toggle staff labels and harmonica labels independently, then switch letters to solfège.
- Compare Listen and virtual playback with a harmonica; confirm both sound harmonica-like.
- In Flow, play one short and one long note; confirm visibly and in scoring that durations differ.
- Complete the rhythm pattern once in Step and once In time; confirm rests and release timing are understandable.
- Open Learn a song through the card library and confirm the staff plus current instrument position stay fully visible.

## Phone (about 5 minutes)

- Check portrait and landscape; press cells in both six-hole banks and verify no page scroll while held.
- Allow microphone; test low/middle/high and one bend trace.
- Run a short flow phrase, background/return the browser, and confirm recovery or a clear restart.

Record browser, OS/device, microphone, confused note, perceived latency, trace clarity, grid comfort, and whether timing/intonation felt too strict.
