# Manual release checklist

Automated Chromium covers Mode A, score step, relative-ear anchor, synthetic pitch lab, mobile layout, and simultaneous slide/hole pointers. These short checks still require a person and physical instrument.

## Laptop (about 5 minutes)

- Open production URL, allow microphone, and confirm low/middle/high note name plus cents.
- Bend slowly into one target; confirm continuous trace and acceptance after stable arrival.
- Complete five Mode A targets, one step phrase, one flow run, and one relative-ear phrase.
- Deny microphone and confirm Virtual remains usable.

## Phone (about 5 minutes)

- Check portrait and landscape; hold Slide with one finger and a hole with another; verify no page scroll.
- Allow microphone; test low/middle/high and one bend trace.
- Run a short flow phrase, background/return the browser, and confirm recovery or a clear restart.

Record device/browser, confused notes, perceived latency, trace clarity, slide comfort, and whether timing/intonation felt too strict.
